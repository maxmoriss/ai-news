### 🤖 AI Agents Enter the Marketplace
In a fascinating step toward autonomous AI economies, **Anthropic** has run an experiment where its AI agents acted as both buyers and sellers on a classified marketplace. The agents negotiated and struck real deals for physical goods, exchanging real money in the process. This test moves beyond theoretical simulations, exploring how AI might eventually handle complex, value-based transactions independently.
[Read more on TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/25/anthropic-created-a-test-marketplace-for-agent-on-agent-commerce/)

### 🇪🇺 A Sovereign AI Challenger Forms
In a significant consolidation play, Canadian AI startup **Cohere** is merging with Germany's **Aleph Alpha**. Backed by the Schwarz Group (owner of Lidl) and with government support, the combined entity aims to create a sovereign European alternative to the US-dominated AI landscape, offering enterprises a new choice for foundational models and services.
[Read more on TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/25/why-cohere-is-merging-with-aleph-alpha/)

### ⚖️ Policy & Accountability
**OpenAI's** role in society faces intense scrutiny as CEO Sam Altman issued a public apology to the community of Tumbler Ridge, Canada. He expressed deep regret that the company failed to alert law enforcement about a suspect involved in a recent mass shooting, highlighting the growing ethical and operational responsibilities of AI firms beyond their software.
[Read more on TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/25/openai-ceo-apologizes-to-tumbler-ridge-community/)

In related policy news, the Governor of Maine **vetoed a bill** that would have enacted the nation's first statewide moratorium on new data center construction until late 2027. The decision underscores the ongoing tension between rapid AI infrastructure growth and local community and environmental concerns.
[Read more on TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/25/maines-governor-vetoes-data-center-moratorium/)
