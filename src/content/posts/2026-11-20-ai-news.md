### AI's Mathematical Leap & The Human Response
The world of mathematics is reeling from what some are calling a "mathocalypse." An **Anthropic mathematician claims to have solved the prestigious, 167-year-old Riemann Hypothesis with the help of Claude** [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1010051/anthropic-riemann-hypothesis-millennium-prize-problem), a claim that coincides with another **AI-driven result for the same problem** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/20/ai-claimed-a-solution-to-the-167-year-old-riemann-hypothesis-and-a-human-did-too/). The implications are profound, prompting mathematicians at **MIT to consider the future of their profession** [MIT News](https://news.mit.edu/2026/following-mathocalypse-mathematicians-consider-future-1120). Separately, foundational AI research continues, with MIT scientists **enforcing physics-based constraints to create reliable models of chaotic systems** [MIT News](https://news.mit.edu/new-approach-enables-physically-consistent-ai-models-chaotic-systems-1120) and working on **more human-like understanding in multimodal models** [MIT News](https://news.mit.edu/2026/qa-enabling-more-human-interactions-understanding-multimodal-foundation-models-1120).

### Privacy Under Siege: From Supply Chains to Screens
A chilling experiment reveals a major flaw in how we build AI. A researcher **planted 2 million canaries (trackable secrets) into the AI supply chain, exposing widespread leakage of private user data** [Ars Technica](https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/11/a-leaky-ai-supply-chain-is-exposing-secret-user-data-at-an-alarming-rate/). In response, privacy-focused apps are taking defensive measures. **Signal is blocking AI agents like Gemini and Siri from taking screenshots** to read user messages [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1038390/signal-blocking-ai-agent-screenshots-mobile-app). On the security front, **OpenAI detailed its layered approach to combatting "adversarial distillation,"** a technique used to extract model capabilities [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/combatting-adversarial-distillation).

### Policy, Power, and Silicon
The intersection of AI, policy, and infrastructure is heating up. **President Trump threatened a "tremendous tax" on companies that build AI data centers outside the US** [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/policy/1038892/trump-foreign-data-centers-tax), even as domestic restrictions grow. In finance, a unique challenge emerges: **lenders are wary of financing chips destined for space, questioning the collateral for orbital data centers** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/20/chips-can-survive-the-space-environment-can-their-financing/). Meanwhile, **the fallout from Nvidia's Jensen Huang scandal continues** alongside its strong earnings [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/podcast/the-fallout-from-nvidias-jensen-huang-scandal-continues/), and **Anthropic's founders cashed out nearly $2B in its record-breaking IPO** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/19/anthropics-7-founders-cashed-out-nearly-2b-in-record-breaking-ipo/).

The "mathocalypse" isn't just about machines solving problems; it's about our systems—technical, commercial, and political—struggling to keep pace with the power they are unleashing.
