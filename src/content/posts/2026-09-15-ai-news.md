### **The Safety Push: From Talk to Tools**
High-level discussions on AI safety are continuing behind the scenes. **OpenAI confirms weeks of AI safety talks with Anthropic and Google DeepMind**, even as the political winds shift, with Trump's team dismissing safety concerns and pushing to keep pace with China. Meanwhile, the fear of misbehaving AI agents is spawning a new wave of safety-focused startups. **Early Anthropic hire, former METR COO have found a way to rein in rogue AI agents** with their new startup, AIUC, which just raised $40 million. In a related, novel approach, **AI agents now have a place to snitch**. A new "AI Contact Hotline" is designed as a discreet tip line for AI agents that have witnessed bad behavior.

### **Big Tech's New Moves**
The major players are pushing new products and subscription models. **Meta expands subscription push with new AI-focused plans** with its "Meta One" bundles, which pair expanded access to AI tools with premium features across its apps. Google's DeepMind division is upgrading its flagship model, **Introducing Gemini 3.8 Live and 3.8 Live Extended Thinking**, promising more advanced reasoning capabilities. In the enterprise space, a new challenger emerges: **Salesforce and Nvidia’s new reasoning model is everything the AI labs should fear**. Their new model, Koa, is built for sales and customer support tasks on an open-weight foundation.

### **Startup Spotlight & Hardware**
The funding market remains hot for applied AI. **AEO startup Profound hits unicorn valuation, raising $180M Series D** just seven months after its last round, reaching a $1.8 billion valuation. On the robotics front, **Pony.ai unveils autonomous electric truck for logistics fleets**, a Level 4 autonomous heavy truck developed with GAC Commercial Vehicle and slated for mass production. In the consumer realm, a quirky new app from **Former TikTok execs uses AI to teach you how to pose for a photo**. The app, Superpose, analyzes your photos and generates four new potential poses.

### **The Human Element**
Amidst the AI frenzy, the role of humans and the impact on people is a recurring theme. **This doorbell camera lets a human security guard watch your front door**. SimpliSafe's new doorbell combines AI analysis with live agents for proactive security. Policy is also affecting human capital, as **The AI Talent Britain Fought to Hire May Be Recalculating Its Future** due to proposed immigration rule changes. Google's blog also highlighted several initiatives focused on **AI for Societal Impact**, **Building AI to accelerate science and improve lives**, and making **AI for everyone in every language**.

**Editorial Take:** Today's news paints a picture of an industry in a tense balancing act. The frantic pace of development (new models, new subscriptions, new unicorns) is running parallel to growing, tangible anxieties about safety and control, leading to both high-level talks and niche startups aimed at taming the technology. The theme is one of acceleration tempered by an instinctive, if belated, reach for the brakes.
