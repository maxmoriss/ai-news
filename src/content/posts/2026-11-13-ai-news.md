### The Nvidia Saga Escalates
The fallout from the Jensen Huang scandal continues to dominate headlines, moving from salacious details to serious geopolitical and corporate consequences. A **Congressional investigation has been launched** by Democrats, focusing on Nvidia's China sales and alleged dealings with the Trump administration. [Source](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/12/democrats-launch-nvidia-investigation-write-scathing-letter-on-huangs-scheme-with-trump/). Compounding the crisis, new research confirms that **China's most powerful AI models were trained on export-banned Nvidia chips**, highlighting the high-stakes tech war at the center of this drama. [Source](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/13/export-banned-nvidia-chips-are-behind-chinas-most-powerful-ai-models-uh-oh-jensen/). Internally, reports suggest Huang showed little confidence in his successor, describing her as a "deer in the headlights." [Source](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/13/jensen-huang-didnt-show-confidence-in-his-successor-report-says/). The turmoil has global ramifications, with **Israel's VC ecosystem** seeing both risk and potential opportunity for challengers to Nvidia's dominance. [Source](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/13/israels-vcs-see-opportunity-for-challengers-in-nvidias-turmoil/).

### Agents Get to Work (and Shop)
While one pillar of AI wobbles, the practical application of AI agents is accelerating. **OpenAI has launched the ChatGPT Wallet**, a secure system that allows AI agents to make purchases on a user's behalf, starting with a limited rollout to U.S. Pro subscribers. [Source](https://openai.com/index/introducing-chatgpt-wallet). In a similar vein, **LangChain, the AI agent startup, has hit a staggering $3 billion valuation**, boasting 7,000 customers for its agent management platform. [Source](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/13/ai-agent-startup-langchain-hits-3b-valuation/). Other agents are finding their niches: **Anthropic's Claude Duet** is being tested as a surprisingly helpful, if neurotic, consumer assistant [Source](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1032518/claude-duet-ai-agent-hands-on), while **Amplitude detailed how it uses OpenAI's models** to ship product improvements in just 1-2 days. [Source](https://openai.com/business/customer-stories/amplitude).

### New Tools & Infrastructure Plays
Beyond agents, today's news highlights AI's expanding utility. **MIT researchers have developed an AI "copilot"** that collaborates with engineers to design better buildings and bridges, using human feedback to build trust. [Source](https://news.mit.edu/2026/new-ai-copilot-helps-engineers-design-better-structures-1113). In robotics, a company founded by MIT's Rodney Brooks, **Robust AI**, is focused on creating robots that safely collaborate with humans in industrial settings. [Source](https://news.mit.edu/2026/helping-robots-fit-into-human-workplace-1113). On the infrastructure front, **Nebius is testing a bold $40 billion cloud expansion plan** funded by customer prepayments, even as questions about its financing model arise. [Source](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/12/nebius-tests-the-neocloud-playbook-with-40-billion-in-planned-spending/). Elsewhere, **Google Search is getting an upgrade** with Gemini for more complex queries [Source](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/12/googles-latest-search-upgrade-promises-better-answers-to-your-most-complicated-questions/), and **ElevenLabs launched a new meeting note-taking app** for teams. [Source](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/13/elevenlabs-new-app-combines-meeting-note-taking-and-dictation-for-teams/).

**Editorial Take:** Today's news paints a picture of an industry at a crossroads. One narrative is a Shakespearean corporate-political drama centered on Nvidia, underscoring how a single company's fate is entangled with national security. The other is the relentless, pragmatic march of AI integration into workflows, commerce, and design. The latter suggests that even as giants stumble, the foundational wave of AI utility continues to build, funded by massive valuations and infrastructure bets. The two stories may be linked: Nvidia's instability could accelerate the search for alternative hardware and agent platforms, making the next few months a critical inflection point.
