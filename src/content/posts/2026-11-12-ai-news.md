### **Leadership Crisis Rocks Nvidia**
The fallout from Jensen Huang's resignation continues to dominate the AI news cycle. New reports from [Reuters, via TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/12/jensen-huang-was-having-a-third-affair-reuters-reports/), allege a third affair with an unnamed Nvidia employee, deepening the scandal that has already forced the iconic CEO out. The immediate succession plan is in place, with CFO [Colette Kress stepping in as interim CEO](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/12/jensen-huang-is-out-at-nvidia-who-is-colette-kress-his-interim-replacement/). However, the long-term leadership is a subject of wild speculation, even entering the political arena as [President Trump suggested Apple Chairman Tim Cook](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/12/trump-floats-apple-chairman-tim-cook-as-jensen-huangs-successor/) as a potential successor.

The implications extend far beyond corporate gossip. Analysts are outlining [five potential ways Huang's ouster could damage the AI industry](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/12/five-ways-jensen-huangs-ouster-from-nvidia-could-damage-the-ai-industry/), citing his unique influence on everything from chip roadmaps to open-source advocacy. A major question mark now hangs over Nvidia's commitment to open-source AI, including its pending $12.9 billion acquisition of Hugging Face. In response, [Hugging Face's CEO has vowed to keep fighting for open source](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/12/hugging-face-ceo-vows-to-keep-fighting-for-open-source-ai-after-jensen-huangs-exit/) regardless of Nvidia's internal turmoil. Huang has also [agreed to testify before Congress](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/12/jensen-huang-agrees-to-testify-before-congress/) this week, where lawmakers will likely probe the national security dimensions of his alleged affair with a woman suspected of spying for China. For a detailed chronology, TechCrunch has published [a full timeline of the unfolding scandal](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/12/nvidia-ceo-jensen-huangs-affair-with-alleged-chinese-spy-a-timeline/).

### **AI Agents: From Hype to Reality (and Friction)**
While Nvidia grapples with its crisis, the practical application of AI agents is accelerating. Google made a major push today, announcing [Gemini 4 Hydrogen](https://deepmind.google/blog/introducing-gemini-4-hydrogen/) and integrating it into Search to provide [better answers to complex questions](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/12/googles-latest-search-upgrade-promises-better-answers-to-your-most-complicated-questions/). In the enterprise, analytics company Amplitude detailed [how it uses OpenAI models to ship product improvements in 1-2 days](https://openai.com/index/amplitude) via an autonomous "Wave" agent. Startups are also building agentic workflows: [Nectar launched an AI agent](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/12/nectar-launches-an-ai-agent-to-automate-creator-discovery-and-outreach-for-brands/) to automate influencer discovery and outreach for brands.

However, the rise of agents is creating new friction points. [United Airlines has explicitly blocked AI agents from booking flights](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/12/united-airlines-blocks-ai-agents-from-booking-flights-citing-cybersecurity-risks/), citing cybersecurity risks. Furthermore, a cautionary tale emerged as a claim that [AI agents discovered a new Maya site was debunked](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1032125/ai-agents-maya-site-archeology-discovery) by The Verge; the site had already been documented, highlighting the need for human verification in autonomous research.

**Editorial Take:** Today's news paints a stark picture of two parallel AI worlds. One is shaken by the very human, political, and security-related vulnerabilities exposed at the highest level of its most powerful company. The other marches steadily forward, deploying increasingly autonomous agents into the real economy, where they are already hitting practical and ethical barriers. The industry's technological ambition continues unabated, but its foundational stability is facing an unprecedented test.
