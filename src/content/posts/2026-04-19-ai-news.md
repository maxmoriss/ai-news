### Robotic Fleets Hit the Road
**Tesla brings its robotaxi service to Dallas and Houston** [Link](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/18/tesla-brings-its-robotaxi-service-to-dallas-and-houston/)
Tesla's autonomous ride-hailing network is now live in Dallas and Houston, marking its expansion beyond the initial Austin launch last year. The service, which began operating without safety drivers in January 2026, now forms a three-city Texas triangle, signaling a cautious but concrete step in scaling its robotaxi ambitions.

### Hardware Heats Up: Chips, Shortages, and Deals
**AI chip startup Cerebras files for IPO** [Link](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/18/ai-chip-startup-cerebras-files-for-ipo/)
In a major move for the AI hardware sector, Cerebras Systems has confidentially filed for an initial public offering. The company, known for its massive wafer-scale chips, enters the public market riding high on recent deals, including a partnership with Amazon Web Services and a reported multi-billion dollar agreement with OpenAI.

**The RAM shortage could last years** [Link](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/914672/the-ram-shortage-could-last-years)
The AI boom's insatiable appetite for memory is creating a supply crisis that may not ease until the end of the decade. A new report suggests DRAM manufacturers will only meet about 60% of demand by 2027, with SK Group's chairman warning shortages could persist until 2030, posing a significant bottleneck for AI development across the board.

### Policy & Ecosystem Shifts
**Anthropic’s relationship with the Trump administration seems to be thawing** [Link](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/18/anthropics-relationship-with-the-trump-administration-seems-to-be-thawing/)
Despite a recent Pentagon designation labeling it a potential supply-chain risk, AI safety lab Anthropic is reportedly maintaining dialogue with high-level officials in the Trump administration. This suggests a potential diplomatic channel remains open, even amidst broader geopolitical tensions surrounding critical AI technology.

**The App Store is booming again, and AI may be why** [Link](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/18/the-app-store-is-booming-again-and-ai-may-be-why/)
A surge of new app launches in 2026 points to a mobile software renaissance, potentially fueled by accessible AI tools. Data from Appfigures indicates that generative AI and other AI-powered features are lowering the barrier to entry and inspiring a new wave of mobile developer activity.
