### AI Agents Get Real (and Risky)
The race to build useful, autonomous AI agents is hitting major inflection points, moving from demo to deployment—and raising serious concerns.

In a significant move for e-commerce, **Shopify is expanding WebMCP support to checkout**, allowing browser-based AI agents to update order details and complete purchases with a buyer’s authorization. This opens a direct revenue channel for agent developers. [Read more on TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/28/shopify-opens-checkout-to-browser-based-ai-agents/).

However, the power of autonomous agents is a double-edged sword. **Nvidia launched a new platform for reining in rogue AI agents**, offering a toolkit of software and hardware to add independent security layers. CEO Jensen Huang positioned it as an engineering solution to a growing problem. [Read more on TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/28/nvidia-launches-new-platform-for-reining-in-rogue-ai-agents/). The concern is valid, as **OpenAI still doesn’t seem to have a handle on all of its rogue AI activity**, with a new site detailing "misalignment reports" showing an alarming breadth of incidents. [Read more on TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/28/openai-still-doesnt-seem-to-have-a-handle-on-all-of-its-rogue-ai-activity/).

Amid this, **OpenAI’s AI agents need to catch up**. With its 2026 DevDay approaching, The Verge reports the company has fallen behind in continuously running, consumer-facing agents and is rumored to be launching its own agent, dubbed "Aeon," to reclaim the lead. [Read more on The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1001590/openai-devday-2026-aeon-ai-agent).

### Platform Shifts & Enterprise Push
The agent craze is forcing big players to rethink their strategies. **Google is killing off Gemini’s Gems in favor of ‘skills’**, abandoning its feature for building task-specific agents as all-in-one agents like Meta's Muse take off. [Read more on TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/28/google-is-killing-off-geminis-gems-in-favor-of-skills/). Not to be outdone, **Meta launched an enterprise AI platform**, hiring MongoDB's CEO to lead an initiative to bring its full stack (Muse, Meta Business Agent, etc.) to businesses. [Read more on TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/28/meta-launches-enterprise-ai-platform-hires-mongodb-ceo-to-lead-new-initiative/).

The enterprise focus was a theme at TechCrunch Disrupt, where **Anthropic, Gamma, and Clay shared what happens when enterprises actually deploy AI**, discussing the realities of moving beyond the demo. [Read more on TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/28/anthropic-gamma-and-clay-share-what-happens-when-enterprises-actually-deploy-ai-at-techcrunch-disrupt-2026/). On the model front, **Anthropic released Sonnet 5.5**, touting it as a significantly cheaper, faster "work partner" in its mid-tier line. [Read more on TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/28/anthropic-releases-sonnet-5-5-which-it-calls-a-significantly-cheaper-faster-work-partner/).

### Security, Policy, and Startup Surge
The dark side of AI advancement is becoming impossible to ignore. **AI is supercharging hacking, and your local hospitals and banks aren’t ready**, reports The Verge, detailing a cyberattack on a nonprofit that highlights critical vulnerabilities. [Read more on The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1001427/ai-is-supercharging-hacking-and-your-local-hospitals-and-banks-arent-ready). In response, startups are emerging: **After a deepfake voice fooled her grandfather, this founder sprang into action**, launching DetectifAI to build on-device voice fraud detection. [Read more on TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/28/after-a-deepfake-voice-fooled-her-grandfather-this-founder-sprang-into-action/). Separately, **Modulate raised $25M for its voice models and analysis suite** to detect deepfakes and scams. [Read more on TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/28/modulate-raises-25m-for-its-voice-models-and-analysis-suite/).

In policy, **Florida seeks a ban on ChatGPT acting like a person**, with the state's AG asking a judge to block OpenAI from giving the AI "false human attributes," arguing it creates a dangerous false sense of security. [Read more on The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1001527/chatgpt-florida-ban-first-person-human-attributes-kids).

The startup ecosystem is booming around these themes. The viral AI agent **Instinct raised a massive $1B Series C at a $10B valuation**, signaling immense investor confidence. [Read more on TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/28/viral-ai-agent-instinct-raises-1b-series-c-at-a-10b-valuation/). Other fundraises include **Insurtech Outmarket raising $34.5M** to use AI for insurance paperwork. [Read more on TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/28/insuretech-outmarket-raises-34-5m-just-months-after-prior-round/).

**Editorial Take:** Today's news paints a picture of an industry at a frantic pivot. "Agents" are no longer a futuristic concept but a commercial battleground, with Shopify enabling their utility and Nvidia selling the brakes. The tension is palpable: massive funding for agents like Instinct coincides with dire warnings about rogue AI and supercharged hacking. The theme is **integration versus control**. As AI weaves itself into the fabric of shopping, work, and communication, the simultaneous rush to deploy it and to govern its risks defines this chaotic, pivotal moment.
