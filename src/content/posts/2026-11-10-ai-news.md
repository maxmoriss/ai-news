### **💰 The Anthropic IPO: A $250M Per Founder Payday**
The AI startup frenzy reached a new zenith as **Anthropic** set its IPO price range, aiming for a staggering valuation of up to $1.404 trillion. In a show of unity, all seven co-founders plan to sell an identical number of shares, netting each approximately **$250 million** at the midpoint price. This move will leave the group firmly in control of the company even as they cash out a significant chunk of equity. [Read more](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/10/anthropic-sets-ipo-price-range-of-70-to-78-per-share-to-reach-an-implied-valuation-of-1-404-trillion/) and [Read more](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/10/all-7-anthropic-founders-will-each-cash-out-about-250m-in-ipo/).

### **🏢 The Feature Wars: AI Gets Down to Business**
The race to become the central AI platform for enterprises is heating up.
*   **OpenAI** is rolling out **verified "Business Agents"** for ChatGPT, allowing brands to create official AI representatives for customer service and commerce. This is part of a broader push with new business profiles to make ChatGPT a "consumer discovery engine." [Read more](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1030552/openai-chatgpt-business-agents-hub) and [Read more](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/10/openai-rolls-out-new-business-profiles-making-chatgpt-just-a-little-bit-more-like-google/).
*   Not to be outdone, **Anthropic** launched **Claude Meetings**, an AI assistant designed to join workplace discussions, draft plans, map timelines, and pull data from connected apps in real-time. [Read more](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/10/anthropic-launches-claude-meetings-to-turn-workplace-discussions-into-plans-and-tasks/).
*   **Google** and **Adobe** announced a deep partnership, integrating Adobe's creative and PDF tools directly into Google Drive and Gemini. Separately, Google Cloud launched **Gemini for Healthcare** to unify clinical data. [Read more](https://blog.google/company-news/inside-google/company-announcements/adobe-google-partnership-integration/) and [Read more](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/infrastructure-and-cloud/google-cloud/gemini-for-healthcare/).

### **🔒 The Nvidia Espionage Scandal: A National Security Threat**
A bombshell report details an alleged affair between **Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang** and a suspected **Chinese spy**, raising profound national security concerns. As the U.S. and China vie for AI supremacy, Huang—whose company's chips are the industry's lifeblood—is described as "one of the least surprising [espionage] targets on Earth." The incident underscores the immense geopolitical stakes wrapped up in the AI hardware race. [Read more](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/10/nvidia-ceo-jensen-huangs-affair-with-alleged-chinese-spy-a-timeline/) and [Read more](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1031063/jensen-huang-ai-race-us-china-spy).
