**Editorial:** This week paints a picture of AI as a force of simultaneous, chaotic creation and disruption. It's solving century-old math problems and promising fusion power, while also threatening to leak our private data and destabilize global governance. The "mathocalypse" is a potent metaphor: AI isn't just a tool; it's becoming a peer—and sometimes a rival—in domains we considered uniquely human. The urgent task is no longer just building smarter AI, but building wiser guardrails around it, a challenge where both the UN and private companies like Signal are currently struggling.
