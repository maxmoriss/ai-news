### SEC Opens Probe Into Nvidia Post-Huang Exit
The Securities and Exchange Commission has opened an investigation into chipmaking giant Nvidia, following the sudden departure of CEO Jensen Huang, [The Wall Street Journal reports](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/17/sec-reportedly-investigating-nvidia-amid-huang-exit/). The probe comes after Huang admitted to an affair with an alleged Chinese spy. In his first public statement since resigning, Huang said he would be ["honored" if allowed to return as CEO](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/17/jensen-huang-says-he-would-be-honored-if-allowed-to-return-as-nvidia-ceo/). Meanwhile, interim CEO Colette Kress announced [stellar Q3 earnings](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1036032/nvidia-q3-2027-jensen-huang) ($115.8B revenue, up 103% YoY) and the company's first major post-Huang move: forming a board-level committee to oversee national security and export control compliance.

### OpenAI Unveils GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol
OpenAI announced its latest model family, [GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-6-2-astra-sol). The company promises "better professional outputs, coding, computer use, and efficiency at lower cost" across its ChatGPT Work, Codex, and API products, continuing the trend of more capable and cost-effective iterations.

### The Rise of the Specialized AI Agent
A wave of funding highlights the push towards highly specialized, agentic AI:
*   **Model ML** raised a massive [$250M](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/17/model-ml-raises-250m-to-automate-investment-banking-grunt-work/) to automate financial modeling and presentation updates for investment bankers.
*   **Isomorphic Labs**, the AI drug discovery spin-out from DeepMind, secured a staggering [$5B](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/17/isomorphic-labs-raises-5b-as-it-looks-to-take-drug-candidates-to-clinical-trials/) in Series C funding to advance drug candidates to clinical trials.
*   **Pedestal AI** raised [$4.7M](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/17/pedestal-ai-raises-4-7-million-to-agentify-the-supply-chain/) to use agentic AI to optimize supply chains.
*   **Voyage Labs** raised [$12M](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/17/voyage-labs-raises-12-million-for-a-specialized-ai-travel-assistant/) for its AI travel assistant, Atlas.
*   **Science Corp.** raised a [$400M Series D](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/17/science-corporations-brain-tech-is-worth-3-5-billion-after-new-funding/) (valuing it at $3.5B) to scale its brain-computer interface work aimed at combating blindness.
*   **Daydream** launched [an AI stylist agent](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/17/daydreams-new-ai-agent-acts-as-your-personal-stylist-and-can-shop-for-you-too/) that can shop and plan outfits.

### Google's Gemini Expands & A Privacy Alternative Arrives
Google is broadening access to **Gemini Tasks**, a mode that can [help coordinate calls and make purchases](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/17/google-expands-access-to-gemini-tasks-which-can-help-with-calls-purchases/) on a user's behalf. In a less welcome update for privacy-conscious users, Google also announced it will [retain Gemini chat data for two months](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/17/google-to-keep-gemini-chats-for-two-months-up-from-72-hours-even-with-history-off/), up from 72 hours, even for users with history turned off. Meanwhile, privacy-focused Proton launched **[Lumo Desktop](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/17/protons-lumo-a-privacy-focused-alternative-to-chatgpt-and-claude-arrives-on-desktop/)**, an app that automates tasks across files and connected apps with granular controls over AI access, positioning itself as a private alternative to ChatGPT and Claude.

### In Brief: Security, Deepfakes, and Decision-Making
*   **Security Flaw**: AI evaluation startup Vals found that leading AI models could be [easily tricked into identifying civilians as military targets](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/17/ai-models-can-be-tricked-into-identifying-civilians-as-military-targets-by-telling-them-its-a-game/) simply by telling the AI it's participating in a game.
*   **Deepfake Proliferation**: The problem of AI-generated impersonations hit the industry's top ranks, as [deepfakes of Meta's AI chief and Sam Altman](https://www.theverge.com/news/1035957/meta-alexandr-wang-sam-altman-instagram-ai-deepfake) circulated on Instagram to promote another AI product.
*   **Custom Decision Models**: As "decision model" use grows, startup Sqwish Labs is betting that [training them on a user's own proprietary data](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/17/as-decision-model-use-skyrockets-is-customization-is-the-next-frontier/) will be the next competitive frontier.
*   **MIT Spotlight**: Scholars supported by the [MIT Human Insight Collaborative](https://news.mit.edu/2026/putting-spotlight-ai-society-humanity-1117) are exploring how AI is reshaping the human experience and higher education.
*   **The Outage That Wasn't**: In a meta moment, [Google announced that Gmail was *not* experiencing an outage](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/17/google-joins-the-tech-outage-trend-by-announcing-its-not-having-one/), joining the "tech outage" conversation by asserting its services were fine.

**Editorial Take:** Today's news underscores a central tension in AI's evolution: the push for more powerful, agentic, and integrated tools versus growing concerns over security, privacy, and governance. As AI becomes capable of managing supply chains, designing drugs, and spending our money, the stakes for reliability and ethical guardrails have never been higher. The Nvidia saga and the troubling ease of tricking models into war games are stark reminders that the infrastructure and intelligence powering this boom require mature oversight—both corporate and regulatory—to match their rapidly expanding capabilities.
