### The Open Source Business Model Under the Microscope
The promise and perils of open source AI are a major theme today. **Hugging Face** is in the spotlight, with its policy chief discussing the costs, risks, and trade-offs for companies building on freely available models. A key question remains: as powerful models become commodities, **[how do the companies behind them profit?](https://techcrunch.com/video/open-source-ai-is-booming-hugging-face-explains-who-profits/)** The discussion also touches on the rising competitive pressure from Chinese open models, challenging American dominance in the field.

### Policy & Backlash: Google's Hardball Tactics
Google is facing significant political backlash for a reported plan that would **turn off Google Search for schools that choose to restrict AI features** like AI Overviews. Critics, including prominent politicians, are accusing the company of monopoly abuse. This controversy has created a direct opportunity for privacy-focused rival **DuckDuckGo, which is now actively pitching its search engine to frustrated schools**, highlighting its option to disable AI features entirely.

### In Other News
*   **Product Rollouts:** **Apple's Siri Recaps** are earning positive, if slightly unsettled, reviews for their utility. Meanwhile, **ChatGPT can now generate animated GIFs**, and **AT&T's smart home security system** is integrating with Google's Gemini for Home.
*   **Hardware & Acquisitions:** **Nothing** teased a screen-free AI device for a December launch. **OpenAI** announced the acquisition of **Pierre Computer Company**, aiming to bolster its code-focused tools.
*   **Infrastructure Strain:** The AI boom's physical limits are showing. **Data centers lost key ballot measures** in the midterms, facing new restrictions. With years-long waits for grid power, some are resorting to using **old jet engines** for electricity—a less efficient and environmentally friendly stopgap.
*   **Market Realities:** **Uber revealed** that third-party AI agents booked only four rides last quarter, a tiny fraction of its business. **Google's parent Alphabet** continues to struggle with monetizing AI as effectively as its core search business.
*   **Security & Identity:** In cybersecurity, **Anthropic is developing "FlashProve,"** a system to cryptographically verify an AI model's identity. Venture firm **Ballistic Ventures** is seeking nearly $500M for new funds, capitalizing on the AI-driven security boom.

**Editorial Take:** Today's news underscores a central tension in the AI era: the clash between aggressive commercial expansion and societal pushback. Google's attempt to force-feed AI into schools feels like a strategic overreach, inviting regulatory scrutiny and empowering alternatives. Meanwhile, the open-source community grapples with the fundamental question of sustainability, proving that even "free" models have significant costs that someone must bear.
