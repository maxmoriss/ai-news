### **Enterprise AI Scales Up**
The drive to embed AI into large-scale business operations accelerated today. **OpenAI is teaming up with global IT services giant Infosys** to bring its AI tools to more enterprises, focusing initially on software engineering and DevOps. This partnership signals a move beyond experimentation to systematic implementation. Meanwhile, **Google deepened its ties with Mira Murati's Thinking Machines Lab** with a new multi-billion-dollar cloud infrastructure deal, and at Google Cloud Next, the company showcased a long list of AI startups it's betting on. In a sign of the times, a consultant noted that **AI in law firms is moving past the hype cycle**, evolving from a novelty to a tool that must now prove its tangible value in expert work.

### **Security & Ethics in the Spotlight**
A significant security incident emerged as **Anthropic confirmed that an unauthorized group accessed its exclusive cybersecurity AI model, Mythos**. The tool, which the company had warned could be dangerous if misused, was reportedly obtained by a third-party contractor. OpenAI CEO Sam Altman called the model's marketing "fear-based." In a separate privacy development, **Meta has begun installing a tool on employee computers** that records keystrokes, clicks, and screenshots to train its AI agents, a move raising immediate workplace surveillance concerns. Additionally, **AI company Clarifai deleted 3 million photos** obtained from dating app OkCupid, following an FTC settlement over data used for facial recognition training.

### **Startups & The Agentic Future**
The startup landscape is buzzing with activity focused on specialized AI applications. **10x Science raised $4.8 million** to help pharma researchers sift through the flood of AI-generated drug candidates. **NeoCognition landed a massive $40 million seed round** to build AI agents that learn like humans. On the infrastructure side, **Google unveiled its 8th-generation TPUs**, announcing two new chips (TPU 8i and TPU 8t) specifically designed to power the coming "agentic era" of AI. In gaming, the makers of AI Dungeon launched **Voyage, a platform for creating AI-powered RPGs** with dynamic NPCs.

### **Big Tech Moves & Model Leaps**
Big tech continues its strategic maneuvers. **SpaceX has a unique option to acquire AI coding startup Cursor for $60 billion**, a potential move to bolster Elon Musk's xAI in the developer tools race. **Google Maps is getting a generative AI infusion**, promising more conversational and intuitive discovery features. And in a display of multimodal progress, **OpenAI's new Images 2.0 model is showing surprising proficiency at generating coherent text within images**, a capability that was notoriously weak just a few years ago.

---
**Editorial Take:** Today's news paints a picture of AI at a crossroads: accelerating into the enterprise mainstream while grappling with the foundational issues of security and ethics that such power demands. The massive funding for "agentic" AI and specialized chips underscores a clear industry bet on what's next—autonomous systems that don't just answer questions but take actions. The Anthropic breach is a stark reminder that as models become more capable, controlling access becomes as critical as building the tech itself. The race is on, but the guardrails are still being built.
