### Google's AI Ad Sparks Independence Day Cringe
In a marketing move that has been widely mocked online, Google released a commercial imagining the Founding Fathers using Google Workspace and Gemini to draft the Declaration of Independence. The ad, featuring Ben Franklin texting Thomas Jefferson, has been described as ["cringey"](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/961468/google-ai-commercial-founding-fathers-declaration-of-independence) and tone-deaf. A separate report notes the ad asks, ["What if the Founding Fathers had access to Google Workspace?"](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/04/new-google-commercial-imagines-a-declaration-of-independence-written-with-help-from-ai/) just in time for the 250th anniversary of the document's signing.

### Corporate AI Clashes: Midjourney vs. Hollywood & Alibaba's Ban
In a significant legal development, image generator **Midjourney** is seeking to compel three major Hollywood studios to disclose details of their own AI usage. This move comes as part of an ongoing lawsuit and could force greater transparency in an industry rapidly adopting AI tools. [Read more on TechCrunch.](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/04/midjourney-wants-hollywood-studios-to-reveal-the-details-of-their-ai-usage/)

Meanwhile, in the corporate world, Chinese tech giant **Alibaba** has reportedly classified Anthropic's **Claude Code** as high-risk software and banned its employees from using it. This highlights the internal security and competitive concerns companies face with the proliferation of powerful AI coding assistants. [Read more on TechCrunch.](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/04/alibaba-reportedly-bans-employees-from-using-claude-code/)

### Community in Conflict: The AI Fanfiction Purge
The fanfiction community is embroiled in a bitter internal conflict over the use of AI. A new movement aims to root out authors using tools like Claude and ChatGPT, but the detection methods are controversial and prone to error, risking false accusations against human writers. The situation illustrates the deep cultural rift and distrust AI tools have created within creative communities. [Read more on The Verge.](https://www.theverge.com/tech/960854/ai-fanfiction-ao3-claude-detector)
