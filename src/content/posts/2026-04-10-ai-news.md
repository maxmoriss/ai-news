### OpenAI Under Scrutiny and a New Price Point
The spotlight is firmly on OpenAI today, but for conflicting reasons. The company announced a new **[$100/month ChatGPT Pro plan](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/09/chatgpt-pro-plan-100-month-codex/)**, positioned between its $20 Plus and $200 Enterprise tiers, offering power users more capacity for coding tasks ([The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/909599/chatgpt-pro-subscription-new)). However, this product news is overshadowed by a significant legal challenge. **Florida's Attorney General has launched an investigation** into OpenAI over national security and public safety concerns, specifically citing a tragic shooting at Florida State University where ChatGPT was allegedly used to plan the attack ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/09/florida-ag-investigation-openai-chatgpt-shooting/), [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/policy/909557/openai-florida-investigation)). This formal probe adds to the intense scrutiny detailed in a new deep-dive on Sam Altman's "messy" tenure and the internal culture at the company ([The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/podcast/909621/openai-sam-altman-drama-vergecast)).

### The Rise (and Limits) of AI Agents
The next frontier of AI is here: systems that don't just answer questions, but take actions. Startups like **Sierra are betting the "era of clicking buttons is over,"** promoting agentic systems that autonomously execute tasks based on natural language commands ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/09/sierras-bret-taylor-says-the-era-of-clicking-buttons-is-over/)). Yet, major players like **Apple and Qualcomm are reportedly building their AI agents with deliberate limits in place**, cautious about unleashing fully autonomous systems ([AI News](https://www.artificialintelligence-news.com/news/why-companies-like-apple-are-building-ai-agents-with-limits/)). This tension highlights a core governance challenge. A new analysis warns that under regulations like the **EU AI Act, the "black box" nature of some agents could create serious accountability problems for IT leaders** ([AI News](https://www.artificialintelligence-news.com/news/agentic-ais-governance-challenges-under-the-eu-ai-act-in-2026/)).

### Market Moves & Model Drama
*   **Meta's AI App Soars, But Philosophy Shifts:** The launch of its new Muse Spark model has sent the **Meta AI app rocketing to No. 5 on the App Store** ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/09/meta-ai-app-climbs-to-no-5-on-the-app-store-after-muse-spark-launch/)). However, critics argue that with its latest competitive models, **Meta is drifting from its open-source identity**, potentially leaving a void in the community ([AI News](https://www.artificialintelligence-news.com/news/meta-muse-spark-ai-model-open-source/)).
*   **Anthropic's Security Dilemma:** **Anthropic is facing questions** over why it limited release of its powerful new "Mythos" model, with some speculating whether cited cybersecurity risks are a cover for other concerns ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/09/is-anthropic-limiting-the-release-of-mythos-to-protect-the-internet-or-anthropic/)).
*   **Infrastructure & Governance:** **Google and Intel are deepening their AI chip partnership** to tackle hardware shortages ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/09/google-and-intel-deepen-ai-infrastructure-partnership/)), while **IBM is making the business case for robust AI governance** as essential for protecting enterprise margins ([AI News](https://www.artificialintelligence-news.com/news/ibm-how-robust-ai-governance-protects-enterprise-margins/)).

**Editorial Take:** Today's news paints a picture of an industry at an inflection point. The frantic pace of product launches (new subscriptions, powerful models, agentic tools) is colliding head-on with sobering real-world consequences and escalating regulatory scrutiny. The Florida investigation into OpenAI is a stark reminder that for all their potential, these systems carry profound risks that the market alone cannot mitigate. The parallel discussions on agentic AI—both its revolutionary promise and its inherent governance nightmares—underscore that the hardest questions about AI are no longer theoretical. They are about accountability, control, and the very real impact on human lives. The era of building first and asking questions later is rapidly closing.
