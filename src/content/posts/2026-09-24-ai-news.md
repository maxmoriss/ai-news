### The AI In Your Pocket (And On Your Wrist)
AI is getting more personal and integrated into daily life. **Meta** unveiled its new AI gadget, the **Muse Charm**. While it looks like a Tamagotchi, its design taps into the Gen Z trend of tech as a fashion accessory, meant to dangle from bags as a discreet AI companion. [Read more on TechCrunch.](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/24/metas-muse-charm-looks-like-a-tamagotchi-but-its-tapping-into-a-much-newer-trend/)

Meanwhile, **Google** is making its AI more useful by testing a feature that lets **Gemini call businesses for you**. Initially available for U.S. Pixel 11 owners with a Gemini subscription, it points to a future where AI handles mundane tasks like appointment booking. [Read more on TechCrunch.](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/24/google-tests-letting-gemini-make-phone-calls-initially-for-us-pixel-owners/)

On the creative side, **Google Photos** has broadly launched its AI-powered **'Clueless'-inspired virtual closet**. The feature automatically builds a digital wardrobe from your photos, helping you plan outfits. [Read more on TechCrunch.](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/24/google-photos-clueless-inspired-virtual-closet-is-now-available-on-android-and-ios/)

### Under the Hood: New Models & Big Money
**Google DeepMind** announced **Gemini 3.8 Live with Live Avatar**, a model featuring a persistent, conversational AI avatar. Separately, Google also launched **Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS** voice models, dedicated speech systems for high-volume audio production in gaming and entertainment. [Read the DeepMind blog.](https://deepmind.google/blog/introducing-gemini-38-live-with-live-avatar/) | [Read more on AI News.](https://www.artificialintelligence-news.com/news/google-gemini-3-8-flash-tts-voice-models/)

Voice AI specialist **ElevenLabs**, now reportedly valued at a staggering **$22 billion**, discussed the ethics of AI voices in customer service. CEO Mati Staniszewski suggested transparency is key, at least until AI calls become the universal expectation. [Read more on TechCrunch.](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/24/twenty-minutes-with-the-ceo-of-elevenlabs-now-reportedly-valued-at-22-billion/)

In startup news, "vibe coding" platform **Lovable** is seeing massive traction, with annualized revenue crossing **$600 million**. [Read more on TechCrunch.](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/24/lovables-annualized-revenue-crosses-600m-as-vibe-coding-takes-off/) Meanwhile, **Ando** has raised $20 million to build a team messaging app designed for humans and AI agents to collaborate, taking aim at Slack. [Read more on TechCrunch.](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/24/ando-eyes-slack-as-it-builds-team-messaging-platform-for-humans-and-agents-to-work-together/)

### Policy & Security: The Scrutiny Intensifies
**Australia** has launched an investigation to determine if **OpenAI** broke the law by allegedly hacking a government health website during data scraping. This marks the first known breach affecting a government agency by the company. [Read more on TechCrunch.](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/24/australia-to-investigate-if-openai-hack-of-government-health-website-broke-the-law/)

In Washington, automated license plate reader company **Flock Safety** faced a tough Senate hearing over its expansive "AI Surveillance Network," with senators criticizing the company's model and its CEO's decision not to appear. [Read more on The Verge.](https://www.theverge.com/policy/1000005/flock-senate-hearing)

A major cryptographic warning emerged: researchers have discovered a **new method to break RSA encryption** that is faster than the traditional factoring approach, potentially undermining a foundational security technology. [Read more on Ars Technica.](https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/09/theres-a-new-way-to-break-rsa-thats-faster-than-anything-weve-seen-before/)

### Infrastructure & Frontiers
**Oracle** has sent a *force majeure* notice related to its planned **New Mexico "Stargate" data center**, a key part of its AI infrastructure partnership with Microsoft. The notice would allow Oracle to delay payments if the facility misses its 2028 target. [Read more on TechCrunch.](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/24/oracle-sends-force-majeure-notice-on-its-new-mexico-stargate-data-center/)

Looking skyward, **Google** is preparing to launch an **AI satellite into space** next week as part of "Project Suncatcher," testing its Tensor processors in orbit for potential future space-based AI data centers. [Read more on The Verge.](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1000015/google-ai-satellite-space-project-suncatcher)

**Editorial Take:** Today's news paints a picture of AI at an inflection point: it's becoming a consumer-friendly product (Muse Charm, Gemini calls) and a powerful, embedded tool (ElevenLabs, Lovable) just as the foundational risks—from cryptographic breaks and rogue agents to regulatory blowback—are coming into sharper focus. The race is on not just to build smarter AI, but to secure it, govern it, and make it a seamless, and trustworthy, part of our world.
