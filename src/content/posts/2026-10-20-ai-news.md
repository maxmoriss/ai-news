### The Rise and Stumble of a Viral AI
Meta's AI companion app, **Muse**, has achieved a staggering growth milestone, surpassing **10 million U.S. downloads in just 40 days**. This puts it in an exclusive club of apps that have grown that fast, a sign of intense public interest in conversational AI. However, this success is immediately tempered by a significant privacy issue. Meta has **paused Muse's conversation-sharing feature** after security researchers found that shared chat links were publicly accessible on the web, potentially exposing personal and business secrets. The company is investigating the flaw, highlighting the persistent tension between rapid feature deployment and user safety in the competitive AI space.

### Policy, Power, and Protocol
Geopolitical and governance concerns are front and center. The **U.S. government has ordered federal civilian agencies to remove China's DeepSeek AI** and related services from their systems, citing national security risks. This move underscores the growing tech cold war dimension of AI development. On the regulatory front, New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani argued that the federal government has "abdicated" on AI, urging citizens to demand protective regulations. Meanwhile, in the tech industry, Figma co-founder Dylan Field commented that the emerging Model Context Protocol (MCP) for connecting AI agents to data is **"not a perfect protocol,"** indicating the ongoing battle to set standards in a fragmented ecosystem.

### Startups & Strange Signals
From the plant kingdom to the meeting room, startups are applying AI in novel ways. **Electric Plant**, a new startup, claims to have built an **AI model that can decipher the subtle electrical signals in plants**, effectively creating a translator for "plant language." In the enterprise world, **Otter.ai raised a massive $320 million** in a unique, non-dilutive funding round to scale its AI meeting assistant. And in robotics, **Eka Robotics** is reportedly wowing experts with a $1.45B-valued robotic arm that uses a "physical AI model" for remarkably dexterous and human-like object manipulation.
