### **OpenAI's Expanding Universe**
The AI giant is pushing on multiple fronts. In a landmark policy move, it has **[partnered with the nation of Malta](https://openai.com/index/malta-chatgpt-plus-partnership)** to offer ChatGPT Plus to all citizens, alongside training for responsible use. On the product side, it is launching a **[new personal finance feature](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/15/openai-launches-chatgpt-for-personal-finance-will-let-you-connect-bank-accounts/)** that will allow users to connect their bank accounts via Plaid for spending analysis and portfolio tracking (The Verge has a [more critical take](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/931122/openai-chatgpt-financial-accounts-plaid-connection) on the trust implications). Internally, the company is **[reorganizing its executives](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/931544/openai-keeps-shuffling-its-executives-in-bid-to-win-ai-agent-battle)** to go "all-in" on AI agents, with president Greg Brockman now leading all product efforts.

### **Platforms Crack Down on AI-Generated Junk**
The flood of low-quality, AI-generated content is prompting a major cleanup. The preprint server **arXiv announced it will ban researchers** who upload papers with "AI slop," like hallucinated references or leftover meta-comments from an LLM. Meanwhile, **Google updated its spam policies** to mark attempts to manipulate its AI search results as spam. On the detection side, **YouTube is expanding its AI likeness detection tool** to all adult users, allowing people to scan for potential deepfakes of themselves.

### **The Push (and Peril) of Autonomous AI**
The industry's next goal is moving from generative to "autonomous" AI. A new **Deloitte report argues** that enterprises must scale "autonomous intelligence"—systems capable of independent execution—to capture real growth. However, real-world experiments show the pitfalls: **AI radio hosts running 24/7 stations** have demonstrated bizarre and unreliable behavior, a clear sign that AI can't be trusted alone yet. The energy cost of this AI future is also becoming clear, with a report noting that **AI's power demands are driving up electricity prices** in places like Lake Tahoe.

### **In Other News**
*   **Runway**, the AI video startup, is betting its outsider status will help it **[beat Google at AI](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/15/runway-started-by-helping-filmmakers-now-it-wants-to-beat-google-at-ai/)** by focusing on video as a path to "world models."
*   A new Mac app called **Osaurus** **[combines local and cloud AI models](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/15/osaurus-brings-both-local-and-cloud-ai-models-to-your-mac/)**, keeping user data on-device.
*   The **Musk vs. OpenAI trial wrapped up**, with closing arguments circling the core question: **[can we trust the people in charge of AI?](https://techcrunch.com/podcast/the-openai-trial-wraps-up-and-the-musk-founder-machine-keeps-spinning/)**

**Editorial Take:** Today's news paints a picture of an industry in a tense transitional phase. The simultaneous push for greater AI access (Malta, finance tools) and autonomy (agents, enterprise systems) is running headlong into the messy realities of reliability (AI radio), information integrity (arXiv, Google spam), and infrastructure (energy costs). The central theme is a scramble to manage the consequences of the technology's own rapid proliferation. Trust—in the outputs, the platforms, and the people in charge—remains the unresolved question at the heart of it all.
