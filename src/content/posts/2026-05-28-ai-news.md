### **The New AI Interface Wars**
The biggest story today is Apple finally showing its hand. Renders and reports indicate a massive redesign for **Siri** is coming with iOS 27, transforming it into a standalone app with a chat interface reminiscent of ChatGPT. This represents Apple's direct entry into the modern conversational AI arena.
*   **Sneak peek at new Siri app reveals Apple’s plans to take on ChatGPT and more** (TechCrunch)
*   **These new iOS 27 renders hint at Siri’s big redesign** (The Verge)

YouTube is also deepening its AI integration for content discovery, launching a feature that lets users **ask an AI to generate a custom video feed** based on descriptions of what they want to watch.
*   **YouTube will let you ask AI to make a custom video feed** (The Verge)
*   **YouTube adds new podcast features, including an AI recommendation tool and ‘Auto speed’** (TechCrunch)

### **The Next Frontier: RSI and Enterprise Maturity**
Beyond consumer products, the industry's ambitions are evolving. A new report explores **Recursive Self-Improvement (RSI)**—the idea of AI systems that can improve themselves—as a potential successor to the AGI (Artificial General Intelligence) hype, though it remains a nebulous and elusive goal.
*   **RSI is the new AGI — and it’s just as hard to pin down** (TechCrunch)

Meanwhile, the enterprise AI market is maturing. At TechCrunch Disrupt, a Databricks co-founder noted the conversation has shifted from excitement to **safety and practical deployment**. This is echoed by startups like payroll service Remote, which reported a 50% increase in revenue per employee through AI adoption without adding headcount.
*   **At TechCrunch Disrupt 2026: Databricks’ co-founder on what kills enterprise AI deals** (TechCrunch)
*   **Payroll startup Remote says it grew revenue 50% per employee without adding headcount** (TechCrunch)

### **Startups, Funding, and Legal Fights**
The startup scene remains vibrant. AI coding startup **Cognition raised a staggering $1B at a $25B pre-money valuation**, showcasing the immense capital flowing into foundational AI tools.
*   **AI coding startup Cognition raises $1B at $25B pre-money valuation** (TechCrunch)

In a notable partnership, **Visa has invested in Replit** to power "agentic payments" for developers, blending AI development platforms with financial infrastructure.
*   **Visa invests in Replit to power agentic payments for developers** (TechCrunch)

However, the legal landscape is heating up. **CNN has sued Perplexity**, alleging its AI tools generate "verbatim" copies of CNN's work and bypass its paywall—a significant copyright case for AI-generated content.
*   **CNN sues Perplexity over ‘verbatim’ copycat articles** (The Verge)

### **Hardware, Subscriptions, and Security**
On the hardware side, **Snowflake signed a massive $6B deal with AWS** for AI CPU chips, and a new company, General Compute, is betting on SambaNova as the next breakout chipmaker. Luxury brand Vertu is also launching an **AI-powered foldable phone** aimed at executives, starting at $6,880.
*   **In more good news for Amazon, Snowflake signs $6B deal with AWS for AI CPU chips** (TechCrunch)
*   **Has the hunt for AI compute uncovered the next Cerebras?** (TechCrunch)
*   **Vertu wants CEOs to run companies from an AI foldable starting at $6,880** (TechCrunch)

Meta is formalizing its subscription strategy, rolling out paid plans for its core apps and testing new **AI-focused offerings** under a "Meta One" brand.
*   **Meta launches Instagram, Facebook, and WhatsApp subscriptions, with more to come, including AI plans** (TechCrunch)

A concerning security report reveals that websites can now spy on visitors by **analyzing their SSD activity** using simple JavaScript, a novel side-channel attack.
*   **Websites have a new way to spy on visitors: Analyzing their SSD activity** (Ars Technica)

### **Editorial Take**
Today's news highlights a pivotal shift: from **speculative hype to tangible product wars and pragmatic business concerns**. Apple's move signifies that the AI interface battle is now on every major platform. Meanwhile, the industry's grand narrative is searching for a new target beyond AGI, perhaps in RSI, while enterprises soberly focus on safe, ROI-driven deployment. The simultaneous explosion in startup funding and escalation in legal challenges underscores that this technology's economic and regulatory foundations are being built—and contested—in real time.
