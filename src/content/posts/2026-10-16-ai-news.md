### The State of Physical AI and Robotics
Amulya Vishwanath, a former Nvidia robotics product lead turned VC, argues that **robotics is in its "Lego era."** In a talk and podcast, she contends the field is still rebuilding fundamental building blocks like actuators, software, and safety standards, pushing back against the notion that a single foundation model will soon trigger a robotics revolution. She also emphasizes that "Physical AI is more than robots," encompassing any AI that interacts with the physical world. [Watch on TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/video/a-nvidia-insider-turned-vc-on-why-robotics-hasnt-hit-its-chatgpt-moment-yet/) | [Listen on TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/podcast/physical-ai-is-more-than-robots-says-the-nvidia-veteran-now-investing-in-it/).

**Editorial Take:** Today's news paints a picture of an industry in a tense race between capability and control. As AI becomes more powerful and embedded—from reasoning models and local chips to ambient devices—the attack surface for misuse (rogue agents, ad-delivered spyware) and the ethical gray areas (data scraping, AI-upscaled games) expand in parallel. The contrasting views on robotics—"Lego era" versus imminent revolution—highlight that even within tech, there's debate over how fast this physical integration will truly happen. The theme is accessibility with consequences: easier access to powerful AI tools also means easier access for bad actors, demanding an equal focus on security and ethical guardrails.
