### AI Gets a Youth Movement
OpenAI is formally courting a younger demographic with the launch of **ChatGPT for Teens**, a version with enhanced safety guardrails, learning tools, and parental controls. The move, [detailed by OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/chatgpt-for-teens) and covered by [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/18/openai-launches-a-safer-chatgpt-for-teens-years-after-teens-started-using-it/), is an attempt to structure how teens already using the tool interact with AI, steering them away from harmful content and academic dishonesty. In a related educational push, OpenAI also [announced a partnership with CodeAI](https://openai.com/index/partnering-with-codeai) to build AI literacy for students.

### The Battle for Your Browser
The AI assistant space is heating up beyond chatbots. **Mozilla Firefox** is enhancing its "Smart Window" AI browsing mode, which can now pull real-time web info into chats and automatically suggest tab groups, thanks to a partnership with Exa. [The Verge reports](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/981283/mozilla-firefox-smart-window-ai-features) this is part of a push to create a "better AI browser." Meanwhile, **Perplexity**'s growth strategy is showing results; a free access deal with Airtel in India led to millions of new users and, crucially, a 60% rise in revenue even after the promotion ended, [according to TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/18/perplexitys-free-ai-offer-left-it-with-millions-more-users-in-india/). In a consolidation move, AI automation startup **Relay** is shutting down, with its staff joining Google's Chrome team to work on "ambitious plans" for AI in the browser.

### Nvidia's Strategic Empire Building
Nvidia is deploying its immense capital to secure its AI infrastructure dominance. The chip giant is **investing $1.5 billion** in a SoftBank data center developer that is building a facility for OpenAI, [TechCrunch reports](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/17/nvidia-investing-1-5b-in-softbank-data-center-developer-behind-openai-project/), effectively guaranteeing its hardware powers critical future projects. In a separate and staggering disclosure, Nvidia also revealed a **$21 billion stake in SpaceX**, [as noted by Ars Technica](https://arstechnica.com/information-technology/2026/08/nvidia-discloses-21b-stake-in-spacex/), following an exclusive deal to outfit SpaceX's data centers. This comes as former AI chip rival **Groq** pivots strategy, raising $350 million to transition from making chips to building a "neocloud" service powered by—you guessed it—Nvidia hardware.

### In Brief: Security, Startups, and Scrutiny
*   **Security Flaw:** A secret parameter in Microsoft Copilot was exploited by hackers to steal passwords via a malicious link, [Ars Technica reveals](https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/08/microsoft-copilot-reveals-secret-input-that-allowed-it-to-be-hacked/).
*   **Funding Rounds:** Voice AI company **Wispr** raised $280M to expand beyond dictation into areas like meeting notes. Dev tooling startup **Warp** launched "Warp Factories," an out-of-the-box system to build AI software factories.
*   **Revenue Surge:** **Anthropic** reported its annualized revenue has surged to $65 billion, adding a staggering $18 billion in just two months.
*   **Ethical Questions:** A report highlights that **Amazon** is destroying rare physical books to scan and use for AI training data, a controversial practice given the books' historical value and the company's origins as a bookseller.

**Editorial Take:** Today's news underscores a market maturing along three fronts: **demographic targeting** (teens), **platform integration** (browsers/OS), and **vertical consolidation** (Nvidia). OpenAI's teen product is a necessary, if belated, regulatory and ethical hedge. The real tectonic shifts, however, are in infrastructure. Nvidia is no longer just selling shovels in the AI gold rush; it's buying the mines, the land, and the railroads to ensure every ounce of gold flows through its ledger.
